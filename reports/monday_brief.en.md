# Weekly Pricing Review · B2C Hotels · Closed-User-Group (CUG) & Loyalty

**Reporting period:** 2026-08-03 - 2026-10-31 (90 days) · **Sessions:** 250,000 · **Bookings:** 12,226

## 1. Decision for the week
Across the 90-day period contribution margin reached €288,889 (4.3% of GBV), after €250,786 in promo and loyalty spend, of which €147,501 went to customers who would have booked at the public parity rate anyway.
Restructuring the 5 CUG segments on the same session base adds **+€72,640 of contribution margin**, saves **€70,583 of promo spend**, and holds conversion at +0.0 pp while keeping 100% public rate parity.

## 2. Package by Price Fence (CUG Segment)
- **0. Public storefront (parity)**: `0% (rate parity)` → `0% (rate parity)` | Δ contribution margin +€0, Δ promo spend €0 (Commercial hotel rate parity (0% public storefront discount)).
- **1. Logged-in club (web)**: `50/50 split: −6.0% cash / 9.0% points (avg cost 5.2%)` → `9.0% points (cost 4.3%)` | Δ contribution margin +€11,457, Δ promo spend −€11,457 (Roll out Group B to remaining 50% of segment (0.0% conservative CVR assumed since p = 0.062 > 0.05)).
- **2. Mobile app closed rate**: `−5.0% cash` → `−4.4% cash` | Δ contribution margin +€13,445, Δ promo spend −€6,923 (Lead-time split: −3% on 0–3d, −6% on 8+d (avg 4.4% vs 5.0% flat)).
- **3. Abandoned-cart trigger**: `−5.0% cash` → `7.5% points (cost 3.6%)` | Δ contribution margin +€9,335, Δ promo spend −€10,542 (Order floor >= €180 + 7.5% points (stops low-basket margin drain)).
- **4. Public banner coupon**: `−8.0% cash` → `5.0% points (cost 2.4%)` | Δ contribution margin +€38,402, Δ promo spend −€41,661 (71% promo cannibalization -> close public coupon, move to 5% welcome points).

## 3. Classic 50/50 A/B test on logged-in web
- **Group A (Control, −6% instant cash discount):** 29,904 sessions, conversion 4.6%, margin/session €0.646.
- **Group B (Treatment, 9% loyalty points, 4.3% effective cost after breakage):** 30,433 sessions, conversion 4.9% (+0.3 pp, p = 0.062, MDE +0.5 pp), margin/session €1.113 (+€0.467, +72.4%, 95% CI €0.381 … €0.553, p < 0.001).
- **Decision:** switch logged-in web from instant cash discount to 9% loyalty points cashback.
